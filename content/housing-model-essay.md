# Households owning and renting accommodation

The bar chart compares the percentages of households living in owned and rented accommodation in England and Wales between 1918 and 2011.

Overall, home ownership became more common over most of the period, while the proportion of households renting declined. The two figures were equal in 1971, and ownership reached its highest level in 2001 before falling in the final year shown.

In 1918, only around 22% of households owned their homes, compared with approximately 78% that rented. The ownership rate rose to about 32% in 1939 and remained unchanged in 1953. It then increased to roughly 41% in 1961, before reaching 50% in 1971. At that point, exactly half of all households rented their accommodation.

By 1981, the proportion of owner-occupiers had climbed to 60%, whereas the figure for renters had fallen to 40%. Ownership continued to rise, reaching approximately 68% in 1991 and 69% in 2001. Over the same years, the rental share fell from around 32% to 31%. However, this pattern reversed between 2001 and 2011, with ownership dropping by five percentage points to 64% and renting rising to 36%.
